Adversarial observations in State-Space Models for Robust RL

CartPole Videos

RL performance under noise, adversarial attack and defense.

Miguel Santos1,2, David Ríos Insua1

1 ICMAT-CSIC, Madrid 2 Escuela de Doctorado de la Universidad Autónoma de Madrid

Video 1

Noisy Kalman filter

Baseline scenario: the controller acts on the state estimated by a Kalman filter from noisy observations. Despite the measurement noise, the filter tracks the cart and pole well enough to keep the pole balanced and close to the desired position.

Video 2

Noisy observations under attack

An adversary perturbs the noisy observations before they reach the filter. The corrupted measurements bias the state estimates, the controller acts poorly, and the pole moves away from the desired position and can no longer be stabilised.

Video 3

Attack with defense (λ = 2.0)

The same attack is applied, but now the defense is active (with strength λ = 2.0). It limits the influence of the manipulated observations on the estimates, so the controller recovers and the pole stays balanced and close to the desired position.
